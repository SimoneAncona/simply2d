The Mario example and animation demo use `mario-sprites.png` and `mario-tiles.png` from
[meth-meth-method/super-mario](https://github.com/meth-meth-method/super-mario/tree/master/public/img).
Their frame coordinates come from the project's
[Mario sprite metadata](https://github.com/meth-meth-method/super-mario/blob/master/public/sprites/mario.json)
and [overworld tile metadata](https://github.com/meth-meth-method/super-mario/blob/master/public/sprites/overworld.json).
The Mario artwork depicts Nintendo characters and is kept within the examples.

`mario.png`, `png_test.png`, and `Roboto-Regular.ttf` were already in this repository.
